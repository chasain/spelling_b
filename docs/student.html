Student guide

A short routine for independent spelling, phonics, high-frequency words, and typing practice.

Daily routine

  1. Open Word Lists and choose the list your adult assigned.
  2. Finish each practice stage. Use the speaker whenever you need to hear a word.
  3. When the stars are full, start a new day next time.

Other practice

Use Phonics and High Frequency for focused word practice. Typing unlocks harder key groups as accuracy and speed improve.

When stuck

Slow down, listen again, and read the correction before retrying. Ask an adult if speech is silent or a word looks wrong.

Student story: “I can practice the right words without needing an adult to explain every screen, and I can see what I finished today.”
