Speech

🔊 Voice quality

Spelling B uses the text-to-speech voice selected by Chrome or your operating system.

ChromeOS — recommended

Open Settings → Accessibility → Text-to-speech → Text-to-speech voice settings. Under the ChromeOS built-in speech engine, install and select an English voice labeled (Natural), then restart Chrome.

ChromeOS may process a Natural voice online. Spelling B does not send speech to the developer; any online voice processing is controlled by the system.

Windows

Open Settings → Time & language → Speech. Choose an English voice, or install Text-to-speech under the English language options, then restart Chrome.

Linux

Chrome uses voices provided by the installed system speech service. Install or select an English text-to-speech voice through your distribution, then restart Chrome. Names and menus vary by distribution.

Optional Chrome AI

Download the language model?

Chrome needs to download and install its on-device language model before Spelling B can generate sentences. This can be a large download and may take several minutes.

Chrome system requirements
  • Windows 10 or 11, macOS 13+, Linux, or a Chromebook Plus.
  • At least 22 GB of free storage on the drive containing your Chrome profile.
  • A GPU with more than 4 GB of video memory, or at least 16 GB of RAM and 4 CPU cores.
  • Unlimited data or an unmetered internet connection for the initial model download.

The model is managed by Chrome and runs on this device. You can cancel and use Import file instead.
