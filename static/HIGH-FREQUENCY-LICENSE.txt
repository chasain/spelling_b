"This documentation, the software and/or database are: Public Domain material by grant from the author, January, 2001."

Spelling B uses a classroom-friendly, de-duplicated selection of 1,000 entries from the general-text and Internet frequency lists. Words are normalized to lowercase, non-word network artifacts and malformed contractions are omitted, and unsuitable elementary-school terms are excluded. Remaining words preserve their order in the source lists.
